# Market and Product Discovery

## 1) Market Analysis

### Context

There is currently strong competition in the job platform market, with many players offering jobs, internships and work-study opportunities.

Despite this abundance, users face several difficulties:

- Dispersion of offers on several platforms
- Lack of customization
- Difficulty quickly comparing opportunities
- Information that is sometimes incomplete or not standardized

In the tech sector, these problems are particularly present for students and young developers.

### Current Trends

- **Centralization of offers** : Users are looking for a single platform
- **Customization by AI** : Smart recommendations become a standard
- **Market Data Analysis** Candidates want to visualize salaries, trends, and skill requirements
- **Simplified user experience** : Quick search and advanced filters

---

## 2) Competitive Analysis

### LinkedIn Jobs
- **Strengths** : Very large offer base, integrated professional network
- **Weaknesses** : Sometimes overloaded interface, unclear recommendations

### Indeed
- **Strengths** : High volume of offers
- **Weaknesses** : Variable quality of ads, not very tech-oriented

### WeLoveDevs
- **Strengths** : Specialized developers, strong technical relevance
- *Weaknesses** : Single source

### Opportunity Identified

No platform combines:
- Multi-source aggregation
- Intelligent analysis
- Visualization of the market
- Custom Recommendations

## 3) User Needs

### Persona 1—Student seeking internship
- **Objective** : Quickly find a relevant internship
- **Frustrations** : Too many inappropriate offers, waste of time
- **Need** : Precise filtering + recommendations

### Persona 2—Junior Developer
- **Objective** : Effectively compare multiple opportunities
- **Frustrations** : Lack of visibility on salaries, vague descriptions
- **Need** : Analytical dashboard

### Persona 3—Alternating
- **Objective** : Identify compatible companies
- **Frustrations** : Scattered information
- **Need** : Centralized view

## 5) Product Choice

### Dashboard
- **Why** : Users waste time navigating between multiple pages
- **How** Create a central dashboard displaying saved recommendations, statistics, and offers
- Interface more complex to design, but improved user experience

### Advanced Filtering
- **Why** : Allow for precise search
- **How** : Multi-Criteria Filters

### Recommendation AI
- **Why** : Reduce search time
- **How** : By choosing these skills, he will be able to recommend the best offers that can match these skills and this will allow him to refine his job search

## 6) Success Assumptions

The project will be considered successful if:
